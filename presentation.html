DESIGN BRIEF

Project Design Brief

Design Challenge
How might we reduce food waste at large weddings by connecting hosts with nearby food-rescue NGOs in real time?
Our Goal: To minimize edible food waste by enabling quick and seamless coordination between wedding hosts and food-rescue organizations.

👥 Target Users

  • Primary Users: Mainly wedding hosts and event organizers
  • Secondary Users: Caterers, event staff, NGOs, and volunteers
  • Final Beneficiaries: People and communities who need food

🎯 Main User Needs

  • A fast and trustworthy way to donate surplus food
  • Real-time NGO connections & rapid coordination
  • Assurance that the food reaches people safely

🔍 Project Scope

  • Connecting hosts with verified nearby NGOs
  • Providing real-time communication & pickup coordination
  • Maintaining food safety standards & protocols
🌟 Project Vision

"A world where no good food goes to waste, and every celebration becomes a reason to feed someone in need."
